# Prince Caspian - Chapter by Chapter Timeline

This document follows the events as they unfold in the book, chapter by chapter, detailing what happens in the narrative sequence.

## Chapter 1: The Island

- The four Pevensie children (Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy) are sitting at a country train station, waiting for their trains to separate schools.
- It's been a year since their previous adventure in Narnia.
- Suddenly, they feel a magical pull and are transported out of the station.
- They find themselves in a dense wood on a beach by the sea.
- They realize they're on an island and begin exploring the coastline.
- The children find an apple orchard and old ruins.
- They discover they are in the ruins of Cair Paravel, their former castle.
- Peter finds an old gold chess piece, which confirms they are in Narnia.
- They deduce that many years must have passed in Narnia since their reign.

## Chapter 2: The Ancient Treasure House

- The children explore more of the ruins and realize they're definitely at Cair Paravel.
- They find the entrance to the treasure chamber, hidden behind ivy.
- They use Edmund's electric torch to explore the underground chamber.
- They discover the chamber is intact with all their old treasures and royal gifts:
  - Lucy's healing cordial and dagger
  - Susan's bow and arrows (but not her horn)
  - Peter's shield and sword Rhindon
- They change into Narnian clothes and retrieve their weapons.
- They realize they need to find food and make a plan.

## Chapter 3: The Dwarf

- The next morning, the children see a boat with two soldiers about to drown a dwarf.
- Susan shoots arrows to stop them; the soldiers flee.
- Peter rescues the bound dwarf from the boat.
- The dwarf is Trumpkin, who initially doesn't believe they are the ancient Kings and Queens.
- To prove their identities, Edmund defeats Trumpkin in a sword match and Susan outperforms him in archery.
- Lucy heals Trumpkin's wound with her cordial.
- Trumpkin tells them they've been summoned to Narnia by Susan's horn, which is now in Prince Caspian's possession.

## Chapter 4: The Dwarf Tells of Prince Caspian

Trumpkin begins telling the children about Prince Caspian:
- Young Prince Caspian lives with his uncle King Miraz.
- Caspian loves his nurse's stories about Old Narnia with talking animals and Aslan.
- When Miraz discovers this, he dismisses the nurse and assigns Doctor Cornelius as Caspian's tutor.
- Cornelius secretly reveals he is half-dwarf and teaches Caspian about true Narnian history.
- He shows Caspian the conjunction of the planets Tarva and Alambil, a good omen.
- Cornelius explains that Telmarines invaded Narnia long ago and suppressed the Old Narnians.

## Chapter 5: Caspian's Adventure in the Mountains

- Queen Prunaprismia gives birth to a son, endangering Caspian's position.
- Doctor Cornelius wakes Caspian at night and tells him he must flee immediately.
- Cornelius reveals that Miraz murdered Caspian's father to take the throne.
- He gives Caspian Queen Susan's magic horn to use in times of greatest need.
- Caspian escapes on his horse Destrier into the woods.
- During a storm, Caspian is knocked unconscious when he hits a tree branch.
- He awakens in a strange dwelling, discovered by two dwarfs (Nikabrik and Trumpkin) and a badger (Trufflehunter).

## Chapter 6: The People That Lived in Hiding

- Caspian learns that Old Narnians still exist in hiding.
- He pledges to restore their rights if they help him claim his throne.
- Caspian, guided by his new friends, meets various Old Narnians:
  - The Three Bulgy Bears
  - The Seven Brothers of the Shuddering Wood (Red Dwarfs)
  - The Black Dwarfs
  - Pattertwig the squirrel
  - Reepicheep and his talking mice
  - Glenstorm the centaur
  - Various other creatures including fauns
- The Old Narnians accept Caspian as their leader against Miraz.
- They establish a council at Dancing Lawn.
- Glenstorm predicts that the time for war has come based on the stars.

## Chapter 7: Old Narnia in Danger

- Caspian's horse returns to Miraz's castle, revealing his escape.
- Doctor Cornelius flees the castle and finds Caspian at Dancing Lawn.
- He warns that Miraz has discovered Old Narnia's existence and is mobilizing his army.
- The Old Narnians retreat to Aslan's How, built over the ancient Stone Table.
- They fortify their position but suffer losses in skirmishes with Miraz's troops.
- A battle led by Caspian and the giant Wimbleweather ends badly for the Old Narnians.
- In the council at Aslan's How, tensions rise as Nikabrik grows desperate.
- Caspian blows Queen Susan's horn to summon help.
- Trumpkin is sent to the ruins of Cair Paravel to meet whoever might come in response.

## Chapter 8: How They Left the Island

- Back in the present timeline, Trumpkin finishes his story about Caspian.
- The Pevensies realize they heard the horn's call at the exact moment they were pulled from the train station.
- The group decides they must reach Aslan's How to help Caspian.
- They take the boat and row to Glasswater Creek, planning to journey inland from there.
- They camp for the night after reaching the creek.

## Chapter 9: What Lucy Saw

- The next morning, they begin their journey through the unfamiliar, overgrown forest.
- They struggle to find their way as the landscape has changed significantly over the centuries.
- They reach a gorge with a river at the bottom (the River Rush).
- Lucy sees Aslan on the opposite side of the gorge, but the others don't see him.
- Lucy insists they should follow Aslan up the gorge, but the others vote to go downstream instead.
- They encounter Telmarine soldiers and are forced to retreat back up the gorge.
- They make camp for the night, exhausted and discouraged.

## Chapter 10: The Return of the Lion

- During the night, Lucy wakes up and sees the trees moving and dancing.
- She follows the movement and finds Aslan.
- Aslan tells her she must wake the others and convince them to follow him, even if they can't see him yet.
- Lucy returns and wakes everyone, telling them Aslan wants them to follow.
- Edmund supports Lucy, but Peter and Susan are reluctant.
- They finally agree to follow Lucy's directions, though most of them cannot see Aslan.
- Aslan leads them to a path down the gorge they hadn't found before.
- Gradually, the others begin to see Aslan too.
- They follow him up the hill to Aslan's How.

## Chapter 11: The Lion Roars

- Aslan roars, awakening the trees and river spirits throughout Narnia.
- The trees join them, appearing in human-like forms (dryads).
- Bacchus, Silenus, and the Maenads also appear and join their company.
- They all feast together in celebration.
- Aslan tells Peter and Edmund to go ahead to Aslan's How while he, the girls, and the revelers will take a different path.
- Peter, Edmund, and Trumpkin arrive at Aslan's How.

## Chapter 12: Sorcery and Sudden Vengeance

- At Aslan's How, Peter, Edmund, and Trumpkin overhear a disturbing council meeting.
- Nikabrik, desperate for victory, has brought in a hag and a werewolf.
- They propose resurrecting the White Witch to fight against Miraz.
- Caspian, Cornelius, and Trufflehunter are horrified and refuse.
- A fight breaks out as Nikabrik and his allies attack.
- Peter, Edmund, and Trumpkin rush in and help defeat the hag, werewolf, and Nikabrik.
- Peter then proposes sending a challenge to Miraz for single combat to decide the throne.
- Edmund is sent as a messenger to deliver the challenge.

## Chapter 13: The High King in Command

- Edmund, accompanied by a giant and a centaur, delivers Peter's challenge to Miraz.
- Two Telmarine lords, Glozelle and Sopespian, manipulate Miraz into accepting the challenge.
- They secretly plan to let Miraz die and blame Peter, allowing them to take control.
- The dueling ground is prepared, with marshals appointed from both sides.
- Peter and Miraz begin their duel while both armies watch.
- After intense fighting, Peter wounds Miraz.
- Miraz trips and falls during the fight.
- The Telmarine lords kill the fallen Miraz with Susan's arrow and blame Peter.
- A battle erupts as the Telmarines attack.
- The Old Narnian army fights back.
- The awakened trees arrive and frighten the Telmarines.

## Chapter 14: How All Were Very Busy

- Meanwhile, Aslan, Susan, and Lucy liberate Beruna with Bacchus and his followers:
  - They destroy the bridge at Beruna
  - They free schoolchildren from oppressive education
  - They heal a sick old woman (revealed to be Caspian's former nurse)
  - They transform a cruel man into a tree
- The Telmarines flee toward the river but find the bridge destroyed.
- The Telmarines surrender.
- Caspian meets Aslan for the first time.
- Reepicheep is found severely wounded and Lucy heals him, though his tail is missing.
- Aslan restores Reepicheep's tail out of respect for his courage and his mice's loyalty.
- Caspian is officially recognized as King of Narnia by Aslan.

## Chapter 15: Aslan Makes a Door in the Air

- Several days later, Aslan gathers everyone at Beruna.
- Aslan reveals that the Telmarines originally came from Earth - they were pirates who found a passage between worlds.
- He offers the Telmarines who don't wish to live under the new order a chance to return to the island on Earth where their ancestors came from.
- Aslan creates a doorway between the worlds.
- Some Telmarines choose to go through.
- Aslan tells Peter and Susan they are too old to return to Narnia again.
- The Pevensies say goodbye to their Narnian friends and Aslan.
- The Pevensies pass through the door and find themselves back at the train station in England.
- No time has passed in England, though they've spent days in Narnia.
- Edmund realizes he left his new torch in Narnia.
