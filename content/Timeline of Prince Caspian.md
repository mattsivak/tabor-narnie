# Timeline of Prince Caspian

This document presents a chronological timeline of all major events in "Prince Caspian: The Return to Narnia" by C.S. Lewis.

## The Golden Age of Narnia (1000+ years before main story)
- Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy enter Narnia through the wardrobe and defeat the White Witch
- The four children are crowned Kings and Queens of Narnia at Cair Paravel
- They rule during the Golden Age of Narnia for many years
  - They sail on their ship *Mořská krasavice* (Sea Beauty) with a swan figurehead
  - They visit Terebintia, Galma, the Seven Islands, and the Lone Islands
- The four monarchs hunt the White Stag and accidentally return to England through the wardrobe
- Upon their return to England, no time has passed, though they've lived many years in Narnia

## The Dark Ages of Narnia (between Golden Age and Caspian's time)
- After the Kings and Queens disappear, Narnia falls into disarray
- The Telmarines, a race of humans from Earth who entered Narnia through a cave portal from an island in the Pacific Ocean, invade Narnia
- Caspian I (the Conqueror) leads the Telmarines to conquer Narnia
- The Telmarines suppress and drive away the talking animals and magical creatures
- The Telmarines develop a fear of the sea and dense forests
- Talking animals and magical creatures go into hiding or are killed
- The humans forget or deny the existence of Old Narnia's inhabitants

## Recent Events Before Caspian X's Time
- King Caspian IX (Caspian X's father) rules Narnia
- Miraz, Caspian IX's brother, murders the king to seize power
- Miraz initially rules as Lord Regent rather than king
- Caspian's mother (described as kind) dies
- Miraz systematically eliminates nobles loyal to Caspian IX:
  - Belisar and Uvilas are shot with arrows during a hunt (made to look like accidents)
  - The Passarid family is sent to fight giants at the northern border
  - Arlian, Erimon, and others are falsely accused of treason and executed
  - Two lords from Beaver's Dam are declared insane and locked away
  - Seven nobles unafraid of the sea are sent east by ship, never to return
- With all opposition removed, Miraz's followers convince him to declare himself king

## Prince Caspian's Early Life
- Prince Caspian grows up in Miraz's castle
- Caspian is raised by a nurse who secretly tells him stories of Old Narnia
- When Miraz discovers this, he dismisses the nurse
- Doctor Cornelius is appointed as Caspian's tutor
  - Cornelius is revealed to be half-dwarf
  - He secretly teaches Caspian about Old Narnia
  - He shows Caspian the conjunction of the noble planets Tarva and Alambil
  - He explains that this astronomical event is a good omen that might bring change to Narnia

## The Crisis Begins
- Queen Prunaprismia (Miraz's wife) gives birth to a son
- Doctor Cornelius warns Caspian that his life is now in danger
- Cornelius gives Caspian Queen Susan's magic horn and advises him to use it only in greatest need
- Caspian escapes the castle on his horse Destrier
- During a storm, Caspian is knocked unconscious when he hits a tree branch
- He is discovered by three Old Narnians: the dwarf Trumpkin, the dwarf Nikabrik, and the badger Trufflehunter

## Caspian Among Old Narnians
- Caspian awakes in the badger's den and discovers Old Narnians still exist
- He pledges to restore their rights if they help him claim his throne
- Caspian meets other Old Narnians:
  - The Three Bulgy Bears
  - Pattertwig the squirrel
  - The Seven Brothers of the Shuddering Wood (Red Dwarfs)
  - Black Dwarfs
  - Glenstorm the centaur and his sons
  - Reepicheep and his talking mice
  - Moles including Lilygloves
  - Camillo the hare
  - Hogglestock the hedgehog
  - Fauns including Mentius, Obentinus, Dumnus, Voluns, Voltinus, Girbius, Nimienus, Nausus, and Oscuns
- Caspian is proclaimed king of Old Narnia
- The Old Narnians establish a headquarters at Aslan's How (built over the Stone Table)
- Miraz discovers Caspian's escape when his horse returns to the castle
- Miraz sends search parties to find Caspian
- Miraz discovers that Old Narnia still exists and mobilizes his army

## War Preparations
- Miraz brings his army to the edge of the forest near Aslan's How
- The Old Narnians try to resist but suffer losses in skirmishes
- A battle led by Caspian and the giant Wimbleweather ends badly for the Old Narnians
- In desperate need, Caspian blows Queen Susan's magic horn

## The Pevensies Return to Narnia
- One year after their first adventure, Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy are waiting at a train station to return to school
- They feel a magical pull and suddenly find themselves on an island in Narnia
- They discover they are on the island where Cair Paravel once stood, now in ruins
- They realize hundreds of Narnian years have passed since their reign
- They find the treasure chamber with their royal gifts still intact:
  - Lucy retrieves her healing cordial and dagger
  - Susan retrieves her bow and arrows (her horn is missing as Caspian has it)
  - Peter retrieves his sword Rhindon and shield
- They realize they've been summoned by Susan's horn
- They rescue a dwarf (Trumpkin) who is about to be drowned by two Telmarine soldiers
- Trumpkin tells them about Prince Caspian and the current situation in Narnia

## Journey to Aslan's How
- The Pevensies and Trumpkin decide to journey to Aslan's How to help Caspian
- They row to Glasswater Creek and begin their journey inland
- They become lost in the unfamiliar, overgrown forest
- Lucy sees Aslan and tries to convince the others to follow him up the gorge rather than down
- The others don't see Aslan and choose the easier path downstream
- They encounter Telmarine soldiers and are forced to retreat
- That night, Lucy wakes and follows Aslan
- Aslan convinces her to wake the others and try again to lead them
- Though reluctant, the others eventually follow Lucy
- Aslan leads them across the gorge and up to Aslan's How

## Awakening of Old Narnia
- Aslan awakens the trees and river god
- Aslan, Bacchus, Silenus, and the Maenads liberate Beruna:
  - They destroy the bridge at Beruna
  - They free schoolchildren from oppressive education
  - They heal a sick old woman (Caspian's former nurse)
  - They transform a cruel man into a tree

## The Final Battle
- At Aslan's How, Nikabrik, desperate for victory, attempts to summon the White Witch with the help of a hag and a werewolf
- Peter, Edmund, and Trumpkin arrive in time to stop the ritual and defeat the conspirators
- Peter sends a challenge to Miraz for single combat to decide the throne
- Two Telmarine lords, Glozelle and Sopespian, manipulate Miraz into accepting the challenge
- Peter and Miraz duel while both armies watch
- Peter wounds Miraz, who then trips and falls
- The Telmarine lords kill Miraz and blame Peter, triggering a battle
- The Old Narnian army fights valiantly
- The awakened trees arrive and terrify the Telmarines
- The Telmarines flee toward the river but find the bridge destroyed
- The river god is released from bondage
- The Telmarines surrender

## Aftermath and Departure
- Caspian is officially crowned King of Narnia
- Aslan offers Telmarines who don't wish to live under the new order a chance to return to Earth (the island their ancestors came from)
- Aslan creates a door between the worlds
- Aslan tells Peter and Susan they are too old to return to Narnia again
- The Pevensies say goodbye to their Narnian friends
- The Pevensies return through the door to the train station in England
- No time has passed in England, though they've spent days in Narnia

## Notable Objects
- Susan's Horn: Magic horn that summons help when blown
- Lucy's Cordial: Contains healing liquid that can cure any injury or illness
- Peter's Sword (Rhindon): The sword used by High King Peter
- The Stone Table: Sacred place where Aslan was sacrificed and resurrected
- Aslan's How: A mound built over the Stone Table

## Important Locations
- Cair Paravel: Former castle of the Kings and Queens, now in ruins on an island
- Beruna: A ford that became a Telmarine town with a bridge
- Aslan's How: A mound built over the Stone Table, headquarters of Old Narnia
- Dancing Lawn: A meeting place for Old Narnians
- Glasswater: A creek on the eastern shore
